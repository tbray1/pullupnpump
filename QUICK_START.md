## 🎬 5-Minute Demo Flow

### As a Customer:
1. **Login** → https://pullupnpump-tbray1s-projects.vercel.app/auth/login
2. **Add Vehicle** → My Vehicles → Add (e.g., 2023 Toyota Camry)
3. **Add Location** → Service Locations → Add (e.g., "Home" at any address)
4. **Request Delivery**:
   - Click "Request Delivery"
   - Select vehicle and location
   - Choose Regular gas, 10 gallons
   - See instant pricing calculation
   - Submit request
5. **Track Live** → Watch real-time status updates as driver progresses

### As a Driver:
1. **Login** → Use driver@demo.com
2. **Go Online** → Click the "Go Online" button
3. **View Jobs** → See available deliveries sorted by distance
4. **Accept Job** → Click on a job and accept it
5. **Update Status**:
   - Mark as En Route
   - Mark as Arrived
   - Mark as Fueling
   - Enter gallons delivered: 10
   - Complete delivery
6. **See Earnings** → Instant payout calculation (70% of job total)

### As an Admin:
1. **Login** → Use admin@demo.com
2. **Dashboard** → See live stats:
   - Total customers, drivers, revenue
   - Today's performance
   - Active jobs in real-time
3. **Manage Drivers**:
   - Click "Drivers" tab
   - Approve/suspend drivers
   - View driver stats and ratings
4. **Monitor Jobs**:
   - Click "Jobs" tab
   - See all deliveries (active and completed)
   - Track system health

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: How do I create an admin account?**
A: Use admin@demo.com (if created) or ask the project owner to promote your account.

**Q: Why can't my driver account accept jobs?**
A: Driver accounts must be approved by an admin first. Use driver@demo.com which is pre-approved.

**Q: Can I test with real payments?**
A: Payment integration is ready but not active in this MVP. Pricing is calculated but no charges are made.

**Q: Is this mobile-friendly?**
A: Yes! It's a Progressive Web App (PWA) that works great on mobile browsers.
